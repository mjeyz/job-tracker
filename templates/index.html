Our Mission

Built for the modern job seeker

Job hunting is chaotic by nature — dozens of tabs, spreadsheets, and email threads competing for attention. JobTrackr brings order to that chaos with a clean, focused workspace designed to keep you moving forward.

Whether you're applying to your first role or making a mid-career pivot, we give you the clarity to focus on what matters: landing the job.

📋

Application Tracking

Log every application with status, company, role, and notes. Know exactly where you stand at a glance.

📊

Progress Dashboard

Visual stats on your pipeline — applied, interviewing, offers, rejections. Your search, quantified.

🔔

Follow-up Reminders

Never let an opportunity go cold. Set reminders and stay proactive throughout your search.

🔒

Secure & Private

Your data belongs to you. Account-based auth ensures only you can see your applications.

⚡

Fast & Lightweight

No bloat. Just a fast, focused tool that gets out of your way so you can focus on applying.

All in One Place
